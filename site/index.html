2. Method

Measured, not guessed

Luminex runs its own OpenBench instance. A candidate change plays the current engine until a sequential probability ratio test can tell a gain from noise.

  1. 1

    Pull request

    Each change comes with the mechanism it relies on and how it was checked.

  2. 2

    Builds

    Continuous integration compiles the engine for Linux, Windows and macOS and checks that each binary runs.

  3. 3

    SPRT

    Games at 10+0.1 against the current engine, until the test accepts a gain between 0 and 5 Elo or rejects it.

  4. 4

    Merge

    A pass merges. Results inside the error margin are decided openly, numbers in the commit.

3. Inside the engine

Two evaluations, one search

Luminex pairs a quantized neural network with a hand-crafted fallback, behind an alpha-beta search that orders, reduces and prunes its moves.

2 × 76816321

A network that follows the pieces

A 768-wide neural network scores every position. A move changes only a few of its inputs, so the first layer is updated with a few additions instead of being computed again. In training, main's network, gen768t_05q, was shown about 1.24 trillion positions in V5's five runs.

best linereducedpruned

A search that knows where to look

Alpha-beta search that orders its moves, searches the best guess in full, reduces the late ones and prunes what cannot matter, so the time goes where the game is decided.

More than a million positions a second

On one thread of an Intel Xeon Platinum 8562Y+, in the games of test 174 at 10+0.1: V5's first network, built with clang 23. Speed is strength at fast time controls.

4. Chronicle

From first commit to 3640

Ten months and 1,779 commits, from a handcrafted engine to a neural one, and every step since November measured on OpenBench

  1. Jan 2026

    First commit

    27 January: a handcrafted evaluation and an alpha-beta search.

  2. Apr 2026

    Version 5

    The hand-crafted evaluation is rewritten (v5.0, 10 April) and tuned release by release: its features follow Stash's, its values are fitted for Luminex.

  3. Jul 2026

    The first network

    12 July: Luminex plays with an NNUE for the first time. SIMD work then takes the network from 81,000 to 982,000 nodes per second.

  4. Aug 2026

    The handcrafted peak ≈2500

    Tuned by distillation, the classical evaluation reaches about 2500 on the Stash ladder (v5.16).

  5. Sep 2026

    gen768 ≈2800

    A 768-wide network becomes the default and NNUE is switched on for everyone (v6.1).

  6. Oct 2026

    3000 crossed ≈3030

    v6.3 ships a network trained on Leela positions labeled by Stockfish, with the loss weighted toward quiet positions.

  7. Nov 2026

    OpenBench, then v6.4

    Every change to the engine's play now goes through an SPRT on a pool of Xeon workers. v6.4 ships the first week of them: the network loads by itself, and every download is built for the CPUs its name promises.

  8. Nov 2026

    v6.5 ≈3255

    A day of search work, each change through an SPRT: history that learns from failed moves, a hash table that keeps its best move, leaner extensions. +145.35 ± 12.02 Elo over v6.4, and Stash 32 beaten. v6.5.1 puts the network inside every download.

  9. Nov 2026

    Time, measured

    After v6.5.1: a time schedule solved from the measured value of time, +72.22 ± 16.84 Elo at 10+0.1, and a stop rule from best-move stability, +8.32 ± 5.29; together +37.46 ± 11.00 at CCI's 60+0.6. Two SPSA runs of 80,000 games each retune the search constants.

  10. Nov 2026

    A new line of networks

    gen768r: eight output buckets, a revived tail, and training through the engine's own rounding. Five networks between 10 and 14 November, on Leela's test91 games, each tested against the one before: +60.08 ± 15.55 Elo with its retuned search constants, then +13.39 ± 6.48, +13.31 ± 6.55, +22.02 ± 8.52 and +3.09 ± 2.46.

  11. Nov 2026

    Development builds and gauntlets

    Every push to main now publishes a development build, and gauntlets on OpenBench rate a build against twelve engines that CCRL rates: 3448 ± 24 on 12 November, 3471 ± 16 a day later with the next network.

  12. Nov 2026

    v6.6 3471

    The gen768r network, time management solved from measured data, a week of search work, and the same trees searched about 9.5% faster. +246.55 ± 13.37 Elo over v6.5.1 in 2,010 games, and 3471 ± 16 on the CCRL Blitz scale from the gauntlet. The network is inside every binary, under its own name.

  13. Nov 2026

    Main, 18 November 3559

    Two networks on from the release: gen768s_01q, its network trained further on the labels of Leela's BT4 network (+77.57 ± 15.21 Elo), then gen768s_03q (+17.80 ± 7.38). Around them: late moves reduced sooner (+6.24 ± 3.94), aligned tables with huge pages (+8.71 ± 4.79), a feature layer whose arithmetic no longer saturates (+16.69 ± 6.86), the move a stopped iteration has already proven (+1.74 ± 1.30), bare-king endings scored before the network, and one binary a platform, built with clang 23. A gauntlet of 8,814 games against eleven engines that CCRL rates puts this build at 3559 ± 12.

  14. Nov 2026

    Main, 20 November 3590

    V5 on main: gen768t_01q reads 55,960 relation inputs a side beside the piece-squares, one piece attacking another or a pawn attacking a pawn. It passed its SPRT against gen768s_03q at +73.37 ± 14.20 Elo (test 174) and +37.91 ± 10.32 against the same training without them (test 173), and its full run, gen768t_02q, added +28.93 ± 9.30 (test 176). Around them: helper threads that search the main thread's own tree, +60.80 ± 11.74 at four threads (test 177), and pondering counted as thinking time on the move (#113). A gauntlet of 8,846 games against eleven engines that CCRL rates puts this build at 3590 ± 11, and against v6.6.0 it scored +212.07 ± 5.22 in 10,024 games (test 178).

  15. Nov 2026

    Main, 22 November 3611

    gen768t_03q, trained 2.61 million steps further with twelve Leela files new to the line, scored +18.53 ± 7.54 against gen768t_02q (test 195). gen768t_04q, 2.50 million steps more on eleven files no run had used, scored +7.22 ± 4.37 against it (test 200) and +6.58 ± 5.68 at 60+0.6 in 2,006 games (test 201), and became main's network on 22 November (#128). The network's activations are now computed in 16-bit integer steps, every evaluation the same and the search 8.7% faster at the AVX2 level (#122). A gauntlet of 8,888 games against eleven engines that CCRL rates puts this build at 3611 ± 8, past 3600 on the CCRL Blitz scale.

  16. Nov 2026

    Main, 23 November 3640

    Two changes fit the network to OpenBench's new EPYC 7702 workers, both computing exactly what main computed: its small layers in vector steps, +6.02 ± 3.82 Elo (test 203), and half the vector multiplies of its second layer, +14.09 ± 6.17 on top of it (test 204). Seventeen defects in the search were fixed in two batches, +10.57 ± 5.33 (test 265) and +15.91 ± 6.80 (test 273), and gen768t_05q, V5's fifth run with t91 back, scored +9.86 ± 5.07 against gen768t_04q (test 264); it has been main's network since 23 November (#130). A gauntlet of 9,024 games against eleven engines that CCRL rates puts this build, main at 3ba03c8, at 3640 ± 8.

  17. Nov 2026

    Six more fixes, and speed

    Six more defects in the search were fixed; their SPRT was stopped undecided at +2.09 ± 2.14 Elo after 23,648 games (test 286) and the fixes merged on the owner's decision (#132). The accumulators' stack is spaced so that no store lands on an address a load just ahead shares its low twelve bits with: the same trees, 1.8% faster on the test workers (#133). Every build warning is gone, and the engine's output is tidier: it names its network at the first search instead of at start-up, repeats the search's last line after a ponder hit, and numbers the best line of MultiPV as the first (#135). No gauntlet has rated them yet.

  18. Nov 2026

    A sharper search, its sources credited

    Five changes to the search passed their SPRTs, each against the engine before it. Good captures after the first move are reduced: +2.93 ± 2.33 Elo (test 293, #137). Quiet checks are reduced too, and a cut node without a hash move takes a deeper internal iterative reduction: +9.06 ± 5.01 together (test 296, #138). Three defects from the search's fourth audit are fixed: +1.57 ± 1.06 (test 303, #140). ProbCut checks each capture in quiescence search before its reduced search: +15.13 ± 6.56 (test 308, #141). A singular move is extended a third ply when its rivals fail far low: +3.30 ± 2.56 (test 309, #149). The bench is now a fingerprint of the search, 44 positions searched to depth 10 that count the same on every build; the one order for tied root moves it needed passed as no regression (test 305, #139), and CI checks each commit's Bench line against it (#146). The license now credits Stash, by Morgan Houppin, beside Stockfish for the code that derives from it (#143). The pins, the slider attack tables, the move list and the legal-move filter were replaced with code of Luminex's own (#144), and so was the UCI layer, which now declares only the options it uses (#145): both search exactly as before. The engine can also load networks with a feature table for each phase of the game, an idea the Triumviratus engine published first, for networks still in training (#148). No gauntlet has rated them yet.
