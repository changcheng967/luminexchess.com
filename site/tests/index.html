Testing The method

Measured, not guessed

Luminex runs its own OpenBench instance. A candidate change plays the current engine until a sequential probability ratio test can tell a gain from noise.

OpenBench's list holds 107 runs, one of them not finished: 76 SPRTs, 23 fixed-length matches, 4 gauntlets and 4 SPSA tuning runs. The SPRTs played 686,772 of the games, the tuning runs 320,000, the matches 52,844 and the gauntlets 28,284; another 4,610 were played in runs since deleted from the list.

Merged without a passing test. Five changes: #5 repaired correctness bugs at a measured cost; #10, #11 and the retuned constants in #57 landed inside the error margin; #69 was stopped undecided at +1.29 ± 2.38 after 24,230 games and went in as a no-regression change. Every other change that failed was closed or dropped.

Not in the record. Test 59, a network trained on a filtered dump that had learned that material is worth almost nothing (6 wins and 79 losses when it was stopped). The fixed-length matches, which are not SPRTs: v6.6.0 against v6.5.1 (test 131) is on the home page, and the rest were release checks and measurements. Test 64, a match of a fixed 3,006 games, is listed all the same. The SEE rewrite, at +127.32, runs off the chart.

Tuning and gauntlets. Four SPSA runs of 80,000 games each retuned search constants; the last, for gen768r_04q (tune 83), moved no margin beyond noise and was not adopted (test 84). Gauntlets rate a build against other engines instead of testing a change: the newest gave 3559 ± 12 on the CCRL Blitz scale for main of 18 November (the gauntlet).

The count of games is OpenBench's own count of every game played, the tuning runs and gauntlets included.
